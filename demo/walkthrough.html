All targets and entity writes in this recording are simulated. Matching values do not confirm physical radar filtering or hardware behaviour.

The LD6004 mapping shows simulated XYZ targets and four detection cuboids. Floor drop-lines show target height. Drag empty scene space to orbit and use the icon buttons to zoom. Click another cube to select its area. Drag the top face to move X/Y and a side face to move height. Drag a vertical edge to resize X/Y together. The height arrow stops at the configured limit.

Enter an exact maximum height of 4.2 metres and choose Apply. The staged draft first stays pending, then the actual simulated bounds agree. A further local height edit is discarded. Fit view includes zones, drafts and targets.

Top view edits the same area's rectangle by dragging its centre or a corner. Pause freezes target motion while numeric editing remains available. Resume starts motion again. LD2450 opens a 2D map and uses direct writes, converting metres to millimetres.

LD6002B shows four detection and four interference areas in 3D. Select Interference 0 and resize its height and X bound with axis arrows. Reset clears local edits and restores the simulated mapping.
